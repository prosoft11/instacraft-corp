About Us

Protuit is an American corporation founded and led by Farris Aurelius, who previously worked at Morgan Stanley Investment Bank in New York and in hedge fund management. For startups and small businesses, Protuit offers all the tools to grow under 1 roof, including marketing, product development, and IT services. Its motto is “building startups as a service”. Whether it is a mobile app, website, or hardware firm – the 30+ specialists at Protuit help startups grow into medium sized companies and build the departments needed to continue that growth beyond. In the heart of Silicon Valley, CEO Aurelius advised entrepreneurs at San Francisco's 500 Startups accelerator and at UnionVC. Among recent projects, CEO Aurelius helped the machine learning startup Bytez.com as a Strategist.
